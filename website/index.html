LESS FUSS. MORE ROOM.

A place for plans.
And not-quite-plans.

Some things have a time. Others just need somewhere to live until you’re ready. Weekaboo makes room for both.

01

Remember it now. Plan it when you’re ready.

Add a task without choosing a date. Pick it up when it’s convenient, give it a deadline, or drag it onto your calendar to set aside time. A little structure, only when you need it.

02

Your calendars. Your contexts.

Group calendars into work, personal, or whatever fits your life—even across accounts. Switch to personal when work is done, or see everything together. You choose what’s in view.

03

Make a change. Keep it in sync.

Create and edit events in Weekaboo, and those changes sync back to your connected calendar. Changes made there come back here, too. Google Calendar, Outlook and iCloud, in one place.

PRIVACY, BY DESIGN

Your week isn’t our business.

The native apps talk directly to Google, Microsoft or Apple. Your calendar data doesn’t pass through a Weekaboo server, and there’s no Weekaboo account to create.

Tasks and cached calendars live in a local database on your device. Your connected calendar providers still handle their calendar data. Export and import your tasks when you want a copy elsewhere.

Open code makes those choices visible. This little website has no analytics, ads or signup form.
