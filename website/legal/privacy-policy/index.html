PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 21 NOVEMBER 2026

A little clarity
about your privacy.

This website

This is a static website. We don’t add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or an email signup form. Its fonts, images and sounds are served with the site. A sound plays only when you tap the mascot. The sample calendar contains fictional plans; checking a sample task doesn’t save or send it anywhere.

The service hosting the site may process connection information, such as your IP address, when it serves a page. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external download or source link takes you to that service.

The native Weekaboo apps

They connect directly to the calendar accounts you choose. Google and Microsoft sign-in use their authorization services; iCloud uses an Apple app-specific password. Calendar requests go to the relevant provider.

There is no Weekaboo server receiving or storing your calendar data, and no Weekaboo account is required. Calendar data is cached in a local database on your device. Native credentials use protected storage. Tasks and preferences stay on your device by default, and task export/import lets you move a copy yourself. Connecting a calendar account does not turn on task sync.

Your calendar providers store the events you sync with them. Their sign-in services and software can also process connection information and authentication diagnostics. Those services follow the privacy policies of Google, Microsoft and Apple. Opening a meeting or map link takes you to that service.

Optional encrypted task sync

Builds that offer encrypted task sync let you separately choose Google Drive as task storage. If you enable it, Weekaboo encrypts task content on your device before uploading it to the app’s private data folder in your Google Drive account. Devices you connect to the same task collection can download and decrypt those changes. This permission does not let Weekaboo browse your ordinary Drive files.

Google stores the encrypted files and can process account, connection and file metadata, such as file sizes and modification times. Weekaboo does not operate a task-storage server. Your devices hold the keys used to open task content. Drive also stores an encrypted recovery record; the recovery file you choose to export contains the material needed to unlock it and should be kept private. Ordinary task exports contain readable task information.

You can pause task sync in the app or revoke Weekaboo’s access through Google. Revoking Google access can also interrupt calendar access for that account. Pausing sync does not delete files already stored in Drive. Google Drive’s app settings let you remove the app’s stored data. Keep a task backup and recovery file before removing data you may need.

Google user data

Weekaboo uses your Google account identity to label the connection, lists your calendars, and reads or changes events when you use the calendar features. Event information can include descriptions, locations, attendees and meeting links. If you separately enable encrypted task sync, Weekaboo also uses your account identity to select the storage account and accesses its own private Drive app-data files. Access is used to provide these features. Weekaboo does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Weekaboo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can choose which calendars appear, disconnect an account in Weekaboo, and revoke access through your provider. Exported task files contain your task information, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Contact and updates

Weekaboo is maintained by Kelly Gold. For privacy questions, email hello@weekaboo.app. If you contact us, your message and reply address are handled by our email services so we can respond; avoid including passwords, access tokens or private calendar details. We update this page when our data practices change, with the revision date shown above.
