A PLAIN-ENGLISH NOTE · SEPTEMBER 2026

A little clarity
about your privacy.

This website

This is a static website. We don’t add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or an email signup form. Its fonts, images and sounds are served with the site. A sound plays only when you tap the mascot. The sample calendar contains fictional plans; checking a sample task doesn’t save or send it anywhere.

The service hosting the site may process connection information, such as your IP address, when it serves a page. Once hosted on GitHub Pages, that hosting is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external download or source link takes you to that service.

The native Weekaboo apps

They connect directly to the calendar accounts you choose. Google and Microsoft sign-in use their authorization services; iCloud uses an Apple app-specific password. Calendar requests go to the relevant provider.

There is no Weekaboo server receiving or storing your calendar data, and no Weekaboo account is required. Calendar data is cached in a local database on your device. Native credentials use protected storage. Your tasks and preferences stay local, and task export/import lets you move a copy yourself. Connecting the same calendar account on two devices does not synchronize Weekaboo tasks.

Your choices

You can choose which calendars appear, disconnect an account in Weekaboo, and revoke access through your provider. Exported task files contain your task information, so keep them somewhere you trust.
